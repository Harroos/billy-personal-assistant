Privacy policy

## Purpose and access

Billy Personal Assistant is a private integration operated by its owner for the owner's authorized accounts. Depending on the permissions granted, it accesses Gmail message metadata and relevant message content, and Google Calendar event details. These are used to identify commitments, review schedules, propose task blocks, and carry out calendar changes the owner approves.

Processing and storage

OpenClaw and its configured AI providers may process relevant email and calendar information to provide the assistant's functions. Relevant excerpts, summaries, proposals, source identifiers, and scheduling state may be retained in assistant history, local files, or the owner's private notes and their configured backups. Those records can persist after Google access is revoked. OAuth credentials are stored separately from public site content in the host's credential storage; they are never intentionally published in these pages.

These public pages contain no mailbox contents, calendar entries, credentials, home/work addresses, or task lists. Information is not sold or used for advertising by this private integration. The owner controls which accounts are connected and which assistant providers and backup services are configured. Those providers' terms and privacy policies also apply to processing by them.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke Google account access in their Google Account's third-party connections settings and pause monitoring in OpenClaw. Revocation prevents further authorized access but does not automatically delete information already retained in assistant history, notes, or backups. The owner can review and remove those records through the relevant local application or backup service.

Google API data

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. This statement does not replace the requirements of those policies or the configured providers' obligations.
