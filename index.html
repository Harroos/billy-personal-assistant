Billy Personal Assistant

Billy is a private, owner-operated assistant for organizing the owner's email commitments, calendar, and personal and work priorities. It runs through OpenClaw. It is not a public service and does not offer public registration.

Google account access

Only accounts explicitly authorized by the owner are connected. The integration requests read-only Gmail access to identify relevant messages and commitments. It also requests Google Calendar access to read schedules and, when the owner explicitly approves, create or update calendar events. Access is limited by the permissions granted for each account and calendar.

Relevant information may be processed by the configured AI providers to interpret commitments and propose scheduling changes. Calendar changes require the owner's approval. The recurring monitor does not send email, accept invitations, or change mailbox contents.
